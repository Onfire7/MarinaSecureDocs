Boats & Vehicles · Page Specification

Vehicle List

Every vehicle kept at the marina — RVs, trailers, cars in leased or reserved spots — searchable by description, plate number, owner, or location.

Purpose

Reached alongside Boat list from the Boats & Vehicles top-level nav item. Vehicles follow the Boat pattern throughout (see Data Model — Vehicle): unrestricted visibility for the vehicle itself, with owner/contact detail gated by the same permissions as everywhere else.

Data shown

FieldSourceNotes
DescriptionVehicle.descriptionThe primary label — staff often only know a vehicle by sight, so this is freeform (e.g. "White Ford F-250").
Plate numberVehicle.plate_numberNullable — not always readable or known; shown plainly as "—" when absent.
Current locationVehicle.current_locationNullable — a vehicle isn't required to have one (e.g. departed, or newly recorded before assignment).
OwnerVehicle.owners[0] (first in succession order)Name only requires view_owner; omitted entirely without it. Owners are optional — a vehicle's owner may not be known.

Actions

ActionGatingBehavior
Open a vehicleNoneNavigates to Vehicle detail.
Add a vehicleedit_owner_contactSame permission as boat creation, per the avoid-permission-bloat principle — vehicles are owner/contact-adjacent records exactly as boats are.
Search / filterNoneBy description, plate number, owner (if view_owner), or location.

States & edge cases

  • No view_owner — the owner column is omitted entirely from the list, not shown blank.
  • Vehicle with no owner recorded — shown plainly; ownership isn't always known and is never mandatory.
  • Vehicle with no plate number — shown plainly with an em-dash; the description carries identification weight instead.
  • Vehicle with no current location — shown with an empty location field, not hidden from the list.

Mobile vs. desktop

Mobile: single searchable list. Desktop: sortable table with plate, location, and owner columns visible without opening each vehicle — matching Boat list.
